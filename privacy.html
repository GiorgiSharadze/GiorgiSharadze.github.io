Privacy Policy – Crowns

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Crowns ("the app") is a logic puzzle game published by Sharikus. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Game data

Your game progress, streaks and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us, and we do not run any servers that receive them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner, occasional full-screen ads between puzzles, and optional video ads you can choose to watch for a hint. Google may collect and use device information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location and ad interaction data to show and measure ads, and, where you consent, to personalize them. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent tool before personalized ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases (iOS)

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive your payment details; we only receive confirmation that the purchase was made.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
